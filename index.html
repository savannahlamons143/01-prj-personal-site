I am a Psychology major and Computer Science minor exploring how human behavior can inform the design, evaluation, and governance of intelligent systems.

My work brings together behavioral research, front-end development, and AI model evaluation. I am especially interested in adversarial behavior, multi-agent dynamics, monitor evasion, and the methods we can use to identify concerning behavior before it causes harm.

Whether I am shaping a user experience or stress-testing an AI system, I bring an observant, interdisciplinary perspective and a willingness to question what a system appears to be doing versus what it is actually doing.
