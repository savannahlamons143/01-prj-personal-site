I am a Psychology major and Computer Science minor exploring how human behavior can inform the design and evaluation of intelligent systems.

My background in behavioral research, AI model evaluation, and front-end development allows me to approach technology from both human and technical perspectives. Through my evaluation work, I have developed and applied assessment criteria, examined model responses, and explored how systems behave under different conditions.

My current research interests focus on multi-agent dynamics, peer-induced behavioral change, structured dissent, and the relationship between monitoring and behavioral safety. I am particularly interested in developing evaluation methods that distinguish genuinely safer behavior from behavior that simply becomes less observable in agent interactions.

Whether I am designing a user experience or evaluating an AI system, I bring an interdisciplinary perspective grounded in psychological inquiry, technical development, and a willingness to question what a system appears to be doing versus what it is actually doing.

Featured Research Proposal · 2026

From Social Influence to AI Safety

Benchmarking Peer-Induced Behavioral Change in Multi-Agent Systems

Research Question

How do peer configuration, structured dissent, access to human escalation, and monitoring visibility affect the adoption, propagation, resistance, and detectability of unsafe strategies in multi-agent systems?

Proposed Methodology

The benchmark uses simulated, permission-constrained environments to examine how evaluated agents respond to different peer configurations, including safe majorities, unsafe consensus, and standardized safety dissent.

Experimental conditions also vary communication pathways, access to human escalation, and the visibility of monitoring mechanisms.

Research Contribution

This proposal connects psychological approaches to social influence with AI behavioral safety. It distinguishes changes in unsafe behavior from changes in the visibility of that behavior to monitoring systems.
